# Residential Energy Guidance

Households with annual electricity consumption above 4,200 kWh should prioritize an efficiency assessment. Recommendations may include insulation, heat-pump optimization, smart controls, rooftop solar, battery storage, and shifting flexible loads to solar-generation or low-demand hours.

Solar generation estimates are directional and depend on roof orientation, shading, weather, location, and system losses. Customer-facing recommendations must clearly state that an installer assessment is required before investment.
